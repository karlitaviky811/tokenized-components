lib-progress-bar

Progress Bar

Indicador de progreso lineal siguiendo la especificación M3 Progress Indicators. Soporta modo determinate (progreso conocido) e indeterminate (duración desconocida). Dos variantes de grosor: baseline (4 dp) y thick (8 dp).

API

InputTipoDefaultDescripción
mode'determinate' | 'indeterminate''determinate'Modo de progreso
valuenumber0Porcentaje (0–100). Solo aplica en modo determinate
thickness'baseline' | 'thick''baseline'Grosor del track: 4 dp (baseline) o 8 dp (thick)

Determinate — baseline (4 dp)

❖ Valores de progreso </>
value: 0 · 10 · 20 · 50 · 80 · 100 · thickness="baseline"
❖ Determinate — thick (8 dp) </>
thickness="thick" · track de 8 dp

Indeterminate

❖ Baseline · Thick </>
mode="indeterminate" · animación continua

Uso

<!-- Determinate -->
<lib-progress-bar mode="determinate" [value]="uploadProgress()" />
<lib-progress-bar mode="determinate" thickness="thick" [value]="50" />

<!-- Indeterminate -->
<lib-progress-bar mode="indeterminate" />
<lib-progress-bar mode="indeterminate" thickness="thick" />

lib-progress-spinner

Progress Spinner

Indicador de progreso circular (M3). Soporta modo determinate e indeterminate. Tamaños: baseline (40 dp, stroke 4 dp) y thick (44 dp, stroke 8 dp).

API

InputTipoDefaultDescripción
mode'determinate' | 'indeterminate''determinate'Modo de progreso
valuenumber0Porcentaje (0–100). Solo aplica en modo determinate
thickness'baseline' | 'thick''baseline'Tamaño y grosor del spinner

Determinate

❖ Baseline (4 dp, 40 px) </>
value: 0 · 10 · 30 · 50 · 80 · 100 · thickness="baseline"
❖ Thick (8 dp, 44 px) </>
thickness="thick" · stroke 8 dp · diámetro 44 px

Indeterminate

❖ Baseline · Thick </>
mode="indeterminate" · arc animado

Uso

<!-- Determinate -->
<lib-progress-spinner mode="determinate" [value]="progress()" />
<lib-progress-spinner mode="determinate" thickness="thick" [value]="75" />

<!-- Indeterminate -->
<lib-progress-spinner mode="indeterminate" />
<lib-progress-spinner mode="indeterminate" thickness="thick" />
